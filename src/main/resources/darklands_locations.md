## Notes on Dynamic Sites

- Village sites may become Castles intermittently or be rolled as such at game-start; they can be razed by good or evil forces over time, or reincorporate as Villages or Castles.
- Castles function in largely the same way.
- Raubritter (robber-knight) Castles can eventually get reoccupied.
- Pagan Altars can be destroyed and may reappear — or fail to reappear if quest objectives are assigned to them.
- "Cave A" is a bandit hideout; "Cave B" holds a more troublesome occupant, a dwarf magician.
- Mines can suddenly become the sites of Mine quests. The site vanishes after the party engages the quest (success or failure) but can reappear.
- "Hidden Dragon Lairs" are invisible and cannot be visited directly, even when a Dragon is active in the area.
- "Hidden Satanic Sites" are invisible and inactive at first, but become possible locations for the Templar Fortress and Citadel. When the Fortress at Lugum is destroyed, another appears at one of these sites. If Baphomet's Citadel is destroyed but Baphomet is not vanquished, he establishes a new Citadel. Hochkönig is part of this rotation; the Lugum site is not.
